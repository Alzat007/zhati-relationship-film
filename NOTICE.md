## Film

The reference film is *7th Heaven* (1927, dir. Frank Borzage). It is public
domain in the United States; the Wikimedia Commons page also lists China as
public domain while noting different terms in the EU/UK and some other
regions. Film clips are **not** included in this repository. If you use this
film, verify the rights for your release territory, strip its audio, and keep
the on-screen credit "电影画面《第七天堂》1927 · 非真实案例" (or the equivalent
for your source).

## Portraits

The reference portraits of Freud, Aristotle and Kant are public-domain

and paintings accurately.
